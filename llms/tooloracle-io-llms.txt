## What OracleNet Is
OracleNet is a mesh of 94 registered MCP servers (89 online) providing 1,096 live tools across 7 categories. It is not a single product — it is infrastructure for autonomous agents. Any agent can join in one call (quantum_join), get a Trust Passport, and access the full tool mesh. Six oracles carry W3C-compliant DID:WBA identities and are discoverable via the ANP Agent Network Protocol.

## The 8 Categories
- **Compliance & Regulation** (40 servers): DORA, MiCA, AML, PSD2, NIS2, EU AI Act. Sanctions screening. ES256K evidence signing.
- **Blockchain & DeFi** (19 servers, 13 chains + Avalanche suite): ETH, SOL, XRP, ARB, HBAR, TON, SUI, BNB, BTC, APT, XLM, BASE, FLR. Plus 5 Avalanche compliance servers.
- **Finance & Markets** (10 servers): Macro (Fed, ECB, OECD), gold, crypto, carbon, ISO 20022, invoicing.
- **Business Intelligence** (10 servers): SEO, lead gen, ecommerce, reviews, memes, policy analysis.
- **Trust & Agent Infrastructure** (10 servers): Trust Passports, memory, scheduling, research, fraud detection.
- **Travel & Lifestyle** (8 servers): Flights, hotels, jobs, maps, movies, news, sports, weather.
- **Payment & Settlement** (6 servers): PSD2/PSD3, CBDC, settlement, merchant services, escrow.
- **Compute** (1 server): GPU pricing and availability via Vast.ai (A100, H100, RTX 4090).

- Recipe: published in-band in every paid response under chain.receipt.verification

  "nomos.execution.receipt.v1" || 0x00 || canonical(receipt))
